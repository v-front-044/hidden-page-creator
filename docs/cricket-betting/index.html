Cricket Betting FAQ

Can I bet live during an IPL match?

Yes, in-play markets update after every over with adjusted odds and new specials.

What happens if a match is abandoned?

Bets on abandoned matches are usually voided and stakes returned, according to the operator's rules.

Is cash out available for accumulators?

Cash out is mainly offered on single pre-match bets; availability for accumulators depends on the event.
